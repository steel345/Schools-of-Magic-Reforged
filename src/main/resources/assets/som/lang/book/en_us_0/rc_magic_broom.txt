  A far finer broom than the sweeping sort. Anointed with flying ointment at the brazier, it rises into a Flying Broom that a magician may ride upon.<~><~>  Set it down and mount it, then hold forward to soar in the direction you look, tilt your gaze up to climb or down to dive. Sprint to hurry along. Only forward answers; the other keys do nothing. Step off and it drifts gently back to the ground.
