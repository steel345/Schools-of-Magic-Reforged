  /bYarrow/z is a wispy yellow flower related to Dandelions.
<~><~>  They are found in /bAncient Forests/z and Ancient Hills growing among the Oaks and Clovers.
<~><~>  Yarrow is connected to /b/aeromancyAeromancy/z magic. Though it is most useful when it is dried and crushed.
