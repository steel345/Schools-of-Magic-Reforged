  /bRoses/z are a fragrant thorny bush with red flowers closely related to Brambles.
<~><~>  They are found in /bFlowery Biomes/z and Dark Forests.
<~><~>  Rose Bushes have a connection to /bEvocation/z magic. Though it’s most useful when they are dried and crushed.
<~><~>  Rose Bushes can be cultivated by right clicking them with Bone Meal.
<~><~>  Rose Bush can be mashed into Red Dye or Rose Extract in a Mortar and Pestle.
