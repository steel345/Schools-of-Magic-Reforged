  The metal /bHandle/z grants the wand a perk. /bCopper/z extends spell effect duration, /bBronze/z increases magical experience gained, and /bBrass/z enhances spell visuals.
<~><~>  /bGold/z deepens elemental specialization, /bSilver/z improves spell accuracy, and /bIron/z quickens cast time and projectile speed.
