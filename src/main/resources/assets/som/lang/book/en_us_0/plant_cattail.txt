  /bCattails/z are an aquatic plant in the grass family known for their sausage shaped spike of dense brown flowers.
<~><~>  They are found in a wide variety of Biomes including the /bRiver Lands/z, the Sinister Swamp, the Strange Hills, and the Acolyte Woods.
<~><~>  Cattail has a natural affinity for /bDivination/z magic. Though it’s most useful when they are dried and crushed.
