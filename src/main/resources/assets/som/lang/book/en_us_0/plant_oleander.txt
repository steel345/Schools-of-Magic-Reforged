  /bOleanders/z are a bush with peachy pink flowers.
<~><~>  They are found in /bAcolyte Woods/z and Mountainous Jungles.
<~><~>  Oleanders have a connection to /bIllusion/z magic. Though it’s most useful when it has been dried and crushed.
<~><~>  Oleanders will grow slowly over time up to three blocks tall.
<~><~>  Oleanders can be mashed into Pink Dye using a Mortar and Pestle.
