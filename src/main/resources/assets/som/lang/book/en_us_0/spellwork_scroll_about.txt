Every scroll needs two things to begin. A /bScroll Seal/z, folded from /bhoneycomb/z and /blapis/z, sits at the centre of each forging. /bSpell Parchment/z, pressed from paper between two sticks, is the blank that activates the work in place of plain paper.<~><~>To forge a higher scroll, place not a Scroll Seal at the centre but the scroll one step lesser in power. The work is still activated with parchment, and rises a single rank each time.
