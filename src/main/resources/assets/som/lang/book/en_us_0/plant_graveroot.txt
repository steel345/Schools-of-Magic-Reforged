  /bGrave Root/z, also called Corpse Root, grows from Grave Grass, typically over the graves of creatures.
<~><~>  Grave Grass is found in /bSinister Swamps/z, Banded Deserts, Desert Canyons, and Desert Uplands.
<~><~>  Grave Root has a natural affinity for /bIllusion/z magic. Though they are most useful when they are dried and crushed.
<~><~>  Grave Root can be harvested from Grave Grass and can be grown on Farmland.
<~><~>  Grave Root can be mashed into Dead Man’s Blood or Grave Root Pulp, both are valuable ingredients.
