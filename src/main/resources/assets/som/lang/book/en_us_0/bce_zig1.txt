  Long ago, an order of Heliomancers who called themselves the Sun Sages raised great stepped pyramids of sandstone. They named them /bziggurats/z.<~><~>  You will not find them in any green and pleasant country. The Sun Sages built in the dry and the barren, half swallowed by /bdesert/z sand or sunk into /bbadland/z clay, where the heat keeps honest folk far away. They are uncommon, so a long road may pass before a stepped shape rises above the flat land.
