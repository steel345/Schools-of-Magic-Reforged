  /bSunflowers/z are tall stalky yellow flowers closely related to Daisies.
<~><~>  They are found in /bFlowery Biomes/z and Plains.
<~><~>  Sunflowers have a connection to /bTransfiguration/z magic. Though it’s most useful when they are dried and crushed.
<~><~>  Sunflowers can be cultivated by right clicking them with Bone Meal.
<~><~>  Sunflowers  can be mashed into Yellow Dye or Sunflower Oil in a Mortar and Pestle.
