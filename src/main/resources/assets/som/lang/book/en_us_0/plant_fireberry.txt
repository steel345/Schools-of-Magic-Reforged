  /bFire Berries/z grow on the Pyracantha bush.
<~><~>  Pyracantha is native to /bAutumn Woods/z and Strange Hills biomes growing among the Bastion Trees and Beanstalks.
<~><~>  Fire Berries is connected to /bAbjuration/z magic. Though it is most useful when it is dried and crushed.
<~><~>  Fire Berries can be harvested from Pyracantha and planted on Farmland to grow more of this herb.
<~><~>  Fireberry Juice, a valuable ingredient in potion making, can be crafted by mashing Fire Berries in a Mortar and Pestle.
