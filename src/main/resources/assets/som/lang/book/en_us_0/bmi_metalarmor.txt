  Beyond iron, you can forge armor and tools from /bSilver/z, /bBronze/z, /bBrass/z and /bSteel/z, each crafted just like its iron version but with the matching ingot.
<~><~>  Silver is enchantable as chainmail, and its blades bite a third deeper into the undead. Bronze carries gold's knack for magic yet far outlasts it. Brass strikes and guards a little harder, but is brittle, shattering when it breaks and wounding the wearer. Steel is the plainest of the five, trading a touch of protection and bite for nothing in return.
<~><~>  Each ingot also answers to a school of magic: /bTenebrium/z for /bevocation/z, Steel for /btransfiguration/z, Silver for /babjuration/z, Brass for /billusion/z, Bronze for /bdivination/z, and /bCopper/z for /bconjuration/z.
