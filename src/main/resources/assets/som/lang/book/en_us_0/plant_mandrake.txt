  /bMandrake Root/z is the root of the Mandrake plant, unsurprisingly.
<~><~>  Mandrakes can be found in /bMountainous Jungles/z and Sinister Swamps among the ferns.
<~><~>  Mandrake Root is connected to /bTransfiguration/z magic. Though it’s most useful when they are dried and crushed.
<~><~>  Mandrake Roots can be harvested and planted on Farmland, it can also be placed on Mud.
<~><~>  Mandrake Root can be mashed into Mud Man Juice and Mandrake Pulp.
