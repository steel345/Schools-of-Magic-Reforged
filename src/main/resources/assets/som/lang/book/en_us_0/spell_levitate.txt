  Levitate lifts a creature or a dropped item off the ground. Hold the spell on it and it rises. Let go and it falls.<~><~>  Once it is a block up you can walk it slowly from side to side. Move it too fast and the hold breaks.<~><~>  Cast it with nothing in front of you and it lifts you instead. You can still walk while you float, but break into a run and you drop.<~><~>  What you can lift goes by the health of the creature.<~><~>  Charge 4 - up to 48 health<~>  Charge 5 - up to 64 health<~>  Charge 6 - up to 100 health<~>  Charge 7 - up to 150 health<~>  Charge 8 - up to 200 health<~>  Charge 9 - anything at all<~><~>  Bosses cannot be lifted by any charge.
