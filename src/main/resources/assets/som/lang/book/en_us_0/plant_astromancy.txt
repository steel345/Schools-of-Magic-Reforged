  /bJimsonweed/z, also called Datura or Moonflower, is a bush with white flowers related to Nightshade.
<~><~>  They are found in /bMountainous Jungles/z, Ancient Dark Forests, and the Acolyte Woods.
<~><~>  Jimsonweed is the herb best suited for /b/astromancyAstromancy/z magic. It’s flowers and berries especially glow with the power of the stars and moon.
<~><~>  Moonberries can be harvested from Jimsonweeds and can be grown on Farmland.
<~><~>  Moonberries can be mashed into Moon Dew, a valuable ingredient.
