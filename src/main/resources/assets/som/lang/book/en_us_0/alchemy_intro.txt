  /bAlchemy/z is the craft of refining raw materials into the potent ingredients a magician needs.
<~><~>  Its two tools are the /bMortar and Pestle/z, for grinding, and the /bCatalyst Basin/z, for transforming reagents into others.
<~><~>  This Manual holds the recorded knowledge of every recipe for the important ingredients you will need throughout your magical journey.
