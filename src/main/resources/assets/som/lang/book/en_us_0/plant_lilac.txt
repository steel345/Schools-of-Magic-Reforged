  /bLilacs/z are a tall purple flower distantly related to the Acolyte Tree.
<~><~>  They can be found in /bPlains/z and Flower Biomes.
<~><~>  Lilacs have a connection to /bDivination/z magic. Though they are most useful when they are dried and crushed.
<~><~>  They can be cultivated by right clicking them with Bone Meal.
<~><~>  Lilacs can be mashed into Magenta Dye in a Mortar and Pestle.
