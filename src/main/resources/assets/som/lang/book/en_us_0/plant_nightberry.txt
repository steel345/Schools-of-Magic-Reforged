  /bNight Berries/z are the fruit of the Nightshade plant.
<~><~>  Nightshade is found in the /bVermilion Grove/z and the Ancient Dark Forest.
<~><~>  Night Berries have a natural affinity for /bEvocation/z magic. Though it’s most useful when they are dried and crushed.
<~><~>  Night Berries can be mashed into Nightberry Juice, a valuable potion ingredient.
