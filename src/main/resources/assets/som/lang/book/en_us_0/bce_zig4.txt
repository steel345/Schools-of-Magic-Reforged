  At the heart of the maze waits the master of the place: a powerful Archdemon, and no small one. It wields both the Infernal magic of its own kind and the Heliomantic magic stolen from the Sun Sages, calling fire and worse upon any who reach its chamber.<~><~>  Within its chamber beats a Demon Heart, and it is that heart, not the stone, that holds the whole place under seal. Take the heart and the binding fails. Whether that is a wise thing to do is a question to answer before you ever draw your weapon.
