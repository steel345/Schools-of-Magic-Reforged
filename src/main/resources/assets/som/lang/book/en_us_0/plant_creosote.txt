  /bCreosote Bush/z, also known as Greasewood, is a desert shrub with yellow flowers.
<~><~>  They are found in /bBanded Deserts/z, Desert Canyons, and Desert Uplands growing among the Brittlebushes and Jubilee Trees.
<~><~>  Creosote Bushes have a connection to /bDivination/z magic. Though it’s most useful when it has been dried and crushed.
<~><~>  Occasionally they are known to be found without their leaves, so keep a sharp eye out.
