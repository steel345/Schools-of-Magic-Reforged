  A /bPlanter/z lets you grow plants on top of any block. Crafted from slabs of any wood type, a little /bdirt/z and /bbone meal/z.
<~><~>  Plants that normally need water beside them, like sugar cane, will grow on a Planter with no water at all.
<~><~>  Lotuses grow on a Planter too, no water required.
