  /bGems/z are an integral component to magic. They can be polished and incorporated into wands; They can be crushed into gem dust and burned in a /bSpell Forge/z to potentially improve one’s understanding of magic; And they can be used in all manner of spells and rituals.
<~><~>  Gem Dust can be created by crushing a gem in a /bMortar & Pestle/z.
<~><~>  A Polished Gem can be crafted by polishing a gem with /bSandpaper/z in a Crafting Table. Sandpaper is crafted using /bPaper/z, /bSand/z, and /bDiamond Dust/z.
<~><~>  Gems, Polished Gems, Gem Blocks, and Polished Gem Blocks are also excellent decorative materials.
