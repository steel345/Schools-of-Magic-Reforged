  A tuft of hay, a stick, and a pinch of unicorn dust bind a helpful spirit into a Broom.<~><~>  Set it down and it tends a ten-block circle: sweeping loose items into nearby chests and barrels, and clearing cobwebs, grass, and ferns. It obeys whoever placed it, guards its ground from foes, and drops all it carries if felled.<~><~>  Sneak and strike it to bid it hold still where it stands; sneak and strike once more to send it back to work.
